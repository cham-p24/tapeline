**Highest-impact on-page item:** `/verify` declares `twitter:card=summary_large_image` but emits no `og:image` and no `twitter:image` — it is the only audited route without an `opengraph-image.tsx` file. Its social/link previews render broken. Fix = add `app/verify/opengraph-image.tsx` (mirror `how-it-works`) **or** pass an `ogImage` arg to the `pageMeta()` call.
