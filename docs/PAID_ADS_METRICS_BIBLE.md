# Paid ads — the metrics bible

*Assembled 2026-08-22; §7, the self-contained Meta section, folded in 2026-08-23. One document answering: how to put paid ads on for Tapeline, what actually converts, and every metric from impression to retained payer — each with where it lives in Tapeline's own stack. Built from a 152-finding verified research sweep (channels, ad metrics, funnel metrics, CRO, attribution, finance policy, fintech benchmarks, plus a repo audit), passed through three verification lenses; every kill and correction from those lenses is applied below, and claims the lenses killed appear only as explicit warnings. This document EXTENDS the standing docs and never restates them: `PAID_MARKETING_PLAYBOOK.md` is the Google build and the ads gate (its §4 governs everything here); `META_ADS_DECISION.md` is the Meta analysis (verdict: don't); `META_GO_LIVE.md` + `META_SAAS_ADS_PLAYBOOK.md` are the runbook and execution layer for the founder-mandated bounded Meta message test — **folded into §7 below in full, the one deliberate exception to the never-restates rule, because Meta is the live channel and this document must stand alone on it** (the runbooks stay the provenance record); `PAID_ADS_PATHWAY.md` carries the worked economics; `SAAS_OPTIMISATION_PLAYBOOK.md` §2.1 holds the stage gate. Where a benchmark here appears to disagree with a decision recorded in those docs, the decision record wins. Nothing here is legal advice.*

*Evidence grades, as in `META_ADS_DECISION.md`: **A** controlled/replicated · **B** large independent dataset · **V** vendor telemetry or vendor survey · **C** practitioner consensus · **D** single case · **E** folklore. **⚠️ marks a metric that lies at small n** — directional at best at Tapeline's volume, never decision-grade. Stale-flagged figures (pre-2023 data) are named as such; use their direction, not their magnitudes.*

*Currency discipline, once, up front: budgets are set in AUD, benchmarks arrive in USD — model in ONE currency. A$1,500 ≈ US$975, which at US$20–30 finance CPMs buys ~33–49k impressions, not 50–75k. The AUD-at-par error silently flatters every reach and cost-per-registration forecast by ~35%.*

## 1. The one-page answer

Two clocks run, and they must not be confused.

**Clock 1 — what spends now.** Exactly one thing: the **Meta bounded message test**, decided by the founder 2026-08-21, fully specified in **§7 below** — self-contained; provenance `META_GO_LIVE.md` + `META_SAAS_ADS_PLAYBOOK.md` — (one campaign, one ad set, `CompleteRegistration`, A$25–50/day held flat, ~3-week burst, pre-registered kill criteria, verdict at burst-end + 7). Paid buys message rankings now and marginal amplification post-gate. ## 2. THE METRICS TREE

Every metric from impression to retained payer, by funnel layer. Reading rules that govern every table:

1. **The DB is ground truth.** `signup_utm_*`, `signup_gclid/gbraid/wbraid`, `signup_referrer_host`, `signup_landing_path`, `trial_started_at`, `stripe_customer_id` survive ad blockers; client-side GA4 does not (an ad-blocker-heavy trader audience loses `gtag.js` entirely, and all four server-side rails are currently dark except the Meta pixel's PageView — repo audit · A · 2026-08-22). When platform and DB disagree, the DB wins.
2. **Blended before attributed.** Numerator from the finance ledger, denominator from Stripe/the users table. Platform-attributed numbers rank creatives and channels against each other; they are never absolute truth (Gordon et al., *Marketing Science* 2023 · A: best observational methods overestimated true lift 3–12× across 663 Facebook RCTs).
3. **7-day-click only** on Meta (Compare Attribution Settings; "First conversion" metric). View-through and the 2026 1-day "engage-through" bucket are the flimsiest evidence class and structurally contaminated by organic/AEO exposure.
4. **Compound thresholds** — time AND spend AND events — never one alone. No per-ad verdict before ~1,000–2,000 impressions and 48 h and 7 days (§7.7 below; provenance `META_SAAS_ADS_PLAYBOOK.md` §7).
5. **GA4 at this scale is a traffic-mix thermometer, not an attribution system.** DDA behaves like last click at ~200 visits/mo (V · 2025), and data thresholding blanks rows under ~40–50 users when Google Signals is on (C · 2026). Fix once: Admin → Reporting identity → **Device-based**, Signals off; the free BigQuery export bypasses thresholds entirely.
6. One currency (preamble).

### 2.7 The liars' list — never decide from these at current n

- **In-platform ROAS and the Purchase column** — structural ~0 (30-day trial vs 7-day windows) plus 20–60% over-credit. Ignore entirely.
- **Any CPA/CPR under 30–50 conversions** — a single view-through registration can halve or double it.
- **View-through and 1-day-engage conversions** — quantify monthly via Compare Attribution Settings; if the view/engage share exceeds ~30% of reported conversions, treat reported CPR as optimistic by roughly that share (Zentric/Loomer · V · 2026).
- **GA4 channel reports** — DDA ≈ last click at this volume; thresholding blanks small rows (fix per reading rule 5); every Facebook click (paid or organic) arrives via the `l.facebook.com` shim, so `signup_referrer_host` cannot split paid from organic — UTMs are the only paid marker and they undercount (in-app browsers, cross-device). Treat UTM-attributed signups as a floor.
- **The Learning Limited badge** — the plan of record is to live there permanently (penalty ~5–10% · C); never restructure to chase it.
- **Ad Strength and Optimization Score** — no correlation with outcomes (Optmyzr · B); dismiss every recommendation and the score can still read 100%.
- **Promo-code redemptions** — capture ~15–21% of the conversions they drive (Podscribe · V · 2025; range corrected across sources). Channel-specific paths/codes are floors and tiebreakers — existence proof, never a kill reason.
- **Any A/B test at ~200 visits/mo** — ship decisions, read pre/post step-changes against the weekly baseline.
- **Retargeting-attributed conversions** — 40–70% overstatement (C), and the pools are below every platform's practical floor at current traffic anyway.
- **Brand-search conversions in CAC math** — pausing brand ads retained 99.5% of clicks via organic in the eBay experiment (Blake, Nosko & Tadelis, *Econometrica* 2015 · A *(older)*). The brand-defence standing exception stands as deliberate insurance (`PAID_MARKETING_PLAYBOOK.md` §4: brand + retargeting capped A$5/day total; A$3/day is the §5.1 brand campaign line), but its attributed conversions are ~non-incremental: exclude them from CAC, never scale brand spend.

### 7.1 Conversion event architecture

**What is built, and its exact state:**

| Piece | State | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Browser pixel (PageView only) | **LIVE 2026-08-22**, dataset **28351455154543230**, marketing pages only | `frontend/components/MetaPixel.tsx` — refuses to render on `/app/*`; `NEXT_PUBLIC_META_PIXEL_ID` is a **build arg** in `frontend/fly.toml` (a Fly *secret* does nothing for `NEXT_PUBLIC_*`) |
| Server-side Conversions API | shipped (#538), **dormant — `META_CAPI_ACCESS_TOKEN` unset** | `backend/app/services/meta_capi.py`; silent no-op until both env vars are set |
| `CompleteRegistration` | wired (fires on signup) | `routers/auth.py` (email) + `routers/oauth.py` (Google) |
| `StartTrial` | wired (fires on write-once `trial_started_at` from the `trialing` webhook) | `routers/webhooks.py` |
| `Purchase` | wired (first charge; idempotent behind the GA4 latch in `stripe_webhook_events`) | `routers/webhooks.py` |
| CSP for Meta origins | shipped | `frontend/next.config.js` |

**The architecture, and why each piece is shaped that way:**

- **The pixel sends PageView and nothing else, on marketing pages only — this is load-bearing, not cosmetic.** Meta's beacon reports the full current URL as its `dl` payload parameter (`Referrer-Policy` does not trim a payload field) and the request goes to facebook.com, so the browser may attach Facebook cookies it already holds. An unscoped pixel would tell Meta **which specific tickers a user researches, linkable to their real Facebook account**. Nothing is lost by scoping: every conversion is server-sent by `meta_capi`, which needs no browser. Honest limit: scoping blocks insertion on `/app/*` hard loads and bookmarks (the normal logged-in case); a client-side navigation from a marketing page into `/app` in the same tab leaves the already-inserted script present, though no new PageView fires.
- **Why `CompleteRegistration` is the optimisation event.** Meta wants ~50 optimisation events per ad set per week to leave learning. Since #536/#548 the trial is card-required, so `StartTrial` is *genuinely scarce* — a strong intent signal precisely because few people do it, and nowhere near 50/week at this volume. `Purchase` is scarcer still and lands ~day 30 — **outside the 7-day click window by construction, and web CAPI rejects events older than 7 days: the in-platform Purchase column reads ~0 regardless of truth** (V). Payers are counted only via the stored-identifier → Stripe join, never from Ads Manager. And never retreat to clicks or landing-page views — click/LPV optimisation routes delivery to accidental-click and Audience Network junk (Loomer split tests · C); that retreat is the documented quality-collapse trap, and optimising clicks with no conversion import is precisely the A$951 failure. Trade-off accepted openly: registration-optimisation finds people who sign up and never pay; the registration → card-wall → `StartTrial` chain in the DB is the quality tripwire (§7.4). All three events flow regardless — which one is optimised toward is a campaign setting, not a code change.
- **Server-only dedupe posture.** Conversions are SERVER-ONLY; the browser pixel never sends a conversion event, so pixel/CAPI dedupe is N/A today. The Events Manager check is therefore simple: **each conversion appears exactly once**. The deterministic `event_id` already derived in `services/meta_capi.py` becomes live only if a browser conversion event is ever added — which would also have to survive the privacy scoping above. Dedupe failure (if that day comes) double-counts registrations and silently halves apparent cost — a false pass of the kill criteria.
- **EMQ expectations.** With today's matching — hashed email + `external_id` only — expect a score of **~5–6**: at Meta's "good" floor (~6), short of the 8+ that high-intent events want (Trackbee/Niblin/CustomerLabs · V/C · 2026). The cheapest meaningful upgrade needs **no new PII**: capture `fbclid` into a first-party `_fbc` cookie at landing and forward `fbp`/`fbc` **unhashed** alongside the hashed email — never hash `fbc`/`fbp`/IP/user-agent (hashing silently zeroes their value). That capture does not exist yet (gap G4): `lib/utm.ts` holds only the gclid family and `users` has no `signup_fbclid` column — clone the shipped `signup_gclid` pattern (capture block, `String(200)` column + migration with an **ID ≤32 chars**, SignupBody field). The `fbclid` → User → Stripe join is also the **only honest Meta payer count** (the 30-day trial guarantees it). Do not collect phone/DOB/address to chase EMQ — a scanner signup has no legitimate reason to hold them, and hashed-email sharing is already on the lawyer's desk (`META_GO_LIVE.md` §10 Q3–5). **Verify EMQ ~48 h after the token goes live, before spend.**
- **Research adds:** Meta's own A/B tests price CAPI-alongside-pixel at 13% lower cost per result (15 tests, 99% confidence), later quoted at 17.8% for web-CAPI adopters — Meta's numbers, treat as a vendor upper bound, direction undisputed (V · 2025). And FPS restricts *targeting*, not CAPI, custom events or server-side signals — this lever is fully available to a finance advertiser.

### 7.3 Creative for conversion

**Concept count and sequencing.** 3–5 **genuinely distinct** concepts — different visual concept + different message + different intended persona. Andromeda's retrieval stage collapses near-duplicates into one delivery entity: six crops of the same screenshot buy one lottery ticket, not six. Every guru volume prescription (8–20+ live ads) is budget-scaled for accounts 10× this size; at A$25–50/day more concepts guarantees unreadable arms, not more learning. **Statics first** — they validate a message for ~5% of video's cost in ~72 h (C); spend founder-video effort only on the 1–2 surviving angles. Launch the full batch at once (submitted as paused ads days early — §7.5), then let delivery run: expect Meta to concentrate 60–80% of spend on an early favourite within days — **that concentration is a test result** (revealed preference), not a malfunction to fight with forced even splits.

**Format table** (every format shows real product UI or plain text — no stock imagery, no AI faces: −8% CVR over $100, −14% over $500 on considered purchases, Columbia/Harvard/CMU × Taboola · B — and a standing footer on every asset: *"Informational only. Descriptive scores, not recommendations."*):

| Priority | Format | Why · notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Text-led static ("one core idea" / anti-ad plain text) | The launch batch. Cold-audience statics need a message, not a product shot; Tapeline's pitch is literally the anti-ad format; the 20%-text-overlay penalty died in 2020. Motley Fool — the closest business-model analog — runs 68% text-led headline ads (Motion · B). |
| 2 | Scanner screen-recording, 15–30 s, phone-recorded | The mechanic-as-hook pattern (Acorns' highest-share format · B); inherently descriptive — "this is what it shows", never "this is what to do". A concrete number in the first 3 seconds lifts finance hook rate ~31% (C) — **descriptive numbers only**: one number 0–100, the 60-second refresh, days tracked. |
| 3 | Scorecard-artifact static | The record as institutional proof the viewer can check. **Show the page's existence and completeness; keep hit-rate/alpha numerals OFF the ad unit** — crop the `/scorecard` per-day table to show the misses and to EXCLUDE the summary-stat strip. The landing page carries the numbers; the ad carries the existence of the record. |
| 4 | Founder-to-camera, 30–60 s, phone-shot | **Graduate winning static messages into this — don't test with it.** No logo/brand font in the first frame, natural light, real background. **Hook benchmarks and reads** (Motion 550k-ad dataset · B · 2026): cold-feed hook rate typically **18–28%, median ~22–23%, <18% = first-frame failure; Reels 24–36%**. **Research corrects, explicitly:** the widely-quoted "25% is table stakes, kill below 25%" line (Billo · C) was reviewed and REJECTED — it sits above the median and would kill par-performing ads; the Motion floors govern, and Tapeline's ads are ranked **against their siblings**, never against a single external cutoff. When a video is weak, fix in order: first frame → on-screen text → product visibility — before rewriting the ad. Read order as data stabilises: hook rate (~1,000 impressions) → sibling-relative CTR (~3,000 impressions; kill at 30–40% under siblings) → cost per `CompleteRegistration` on 7-day click (§7.4). Refresh trigger is **frequency > 2.5–3.0, not the calendar** — at this spend against a US-broad audience fatigue arrives on a 4–8-week cycle; add 1–2 new concepts monthly, and reserve ~10–15% of budget for slower-burn concepts (CTC's median outlier surfaced at day 39 · B).

**Hooks that need no hype** — four of Motion's five winning archetypes survive the linter; the urgency/offer class is forfeited and nothing real is lost, because **Meta's finance review rejects that class too** (return promises, urgency around financial decisions, guaranteed outcomes, wealth imagery): descriptive-only copy is a policy *advantage* — fewer rejections, no account-quality damage — while hype-dependent competitors get filtered.

### 7.7 Pre-registered verdict criteria — in full, written down before the first dollar

1. **Stop immediately** if the domain shows as data-sharing restricted, or if US financial-advertiser verification for investment products arrives — an unlicensed publisher-exemption product cannot verify; the channel closes. Re-check monthly.
2. **Stop at the pre-set spend cap**, whatever happens.
3. **Stop** if cost per `CompleteRegistration` exceeds **~$50 after 100+ clicks**. (Calibration: finance CPL ~$25–60 · B; materially under ~$25–40 would itself be a notable positive.)
4. **Per-ad refinements** (research floors, not competitors to 1–3): compound thresholds per §7.4; the 3× zero-registration structural kill **with the public-page-arm exemption and their separately pre-registered reads**; soft review at 1.5–2× sustained 5+ days; the sibling-CTR kill. **Target cost-per-registration for this kill arithmetic, pinned once: the ~US$50 tolerance line from criterion 3** — the only pre-registered target the burst has (the economics-derived affordable CPR is deliberately neither a bidding nor a kill input, §7.2) — so the structural zero-registration kill trips at ~US$150 (~A$230) spent on a single ad.
5. **The verdict date is burst-end + 7 days** — conversions report on the *click* date, so the trailing week always looks worse than it will finish. Conclusions are drawn per concept-batch, never per ad.
6. **Success** = one message earns registrations materially cheaper than its siblings — **even if every arm is unprofitable**. The ranking is the deliverable; it transfers to AEO copy, email subject lines and comparison-page H1s — the channels that have actually produced engaged users.
7. **Pre-committed interpretations:** zero winners from 3–5 concepts is the base-rate outcome (5–8% of ads become winners; ~29 launches to find an outlier · B) and licenses "these messages didn't clear the bar", never "Meta is disproven". ## 8. The sceptic's box — why paid stays a bad channel until trial→paid is proven

Stated fairly, because the pre-registered criteria only hold if this was read first.

- **The step that's broken is the one no ad touches.** Tapeline's problem is trial→paid: 0 payers ever, ~0 of ~5 legacy trials. Prospecting buys visitor→signup — a step already converting at the SaaS median (~3.5%). Money spent upstream of a broken step measures nothing about the break; the instrument for trial→paid remains interviews and the existing trialists, at $0 (`META_GO_LIVE.md` §9; `SAAS_OPTIMISATION_PLAYBOOK.md` §2.1 gates engineering behind ≥6 interviews — still 0 recorded).
- **Channel-level ROI at this n is not merely hard — it is statistically impossible.** The peer-reviewed benchmark: across 25 large ad experiments with millions of users each, the median ROI confidence interval exceeded 100 percentage points, and finance advertisers were ~4× harder to measure than retail (median standard error 115% vs 26%) because finance conversions are rare and high-variance — exactly Tapeline's shape (Lewis & Rao, *QJE* 2015 · A *(older)*). Anyone selling channel-ROI numbers at 20 users is selling noise.
- **Attribution flatters everything it touches.** The best observational methods, calibrated against 663 real Facebook RCTs, overestimated true lift by 3–12×, worst for lower-funnel events like registrations (Gordon et al. · A · 2023). Formal incrementality is priced out (Meta Brand Lift now $120k minimum; geo holdouts need conversion volume Tapeline won't have this year · C). The only honest instruments are the ones in §6: blended CAC, the weekly step-change, cohort trial→paid, and the HDYHAU field.
- **The economics were never rescued.** Best-case ~$164/payer against a $30–60 ceiling; ~1.85:1 LTV:CAC at founding prices against a 3:1 gate; and the card gate — correctly — trades top-of-funnel volume for intent, which makes per-registration costs read worse, not better. The external comps agree from every side: median finance search CPL ~$74 (B), fin-services blended CAC ~$784 (C), and the one sub-$60 published trading-product CAC (Robinhood's $15–53) was earned at mania peak on 80%+ organic/referral (A).
- **The category's winners did not buy their growth.** TradingView ~68.5% direct; Finviz ~79.2% direct; Benzinga an SEO machine; the indie comp (stockanalysis.com) built on free data pages; the premium incumbents distribute via 15–30% rev-share affiliates (B/V · 2026). Trade Ideas — the "expensive incumbent" itself — is a ~$3–7M, 28-person company: the paid-scanner category is niche, and none of it sustains meaningful paid-media CAC. The affordable-CAC math that fails for Tapeline fails for the whole category, which is why none of them buy ads.
- **Zero winners is the base-rate outcome, and success doesn't scale.** 5–8% of ads become winners; ~29 launches to find an outlier (Motion/CTC · B); the burst funds 3–5 concepts. A passed message test licenses a second message test — the closest founder case saw CAC roughly double within days of scaling (D). Nothing in a good burst readout reopens the `META_ADS_DECISION.md` §7 gate or the `PAID_MARKETING_PLAYBOOK.md` §4 gate.
