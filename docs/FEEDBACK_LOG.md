## What to record — and what never to record

**Record**, for every reply, call, or interview:
- **Date** and **source** (reply to onboarding email · switch interview · support thread · open session)
- **What they used before / would use instead** — the *named* alternative (Finviz free? Trade Ideas? a spreadsheet? nothing?). This is the single most valuable field.
- **The workflow moment** — *when* in their day/week they reach for a scanner, in their words
- **What they hoped to see first**, and whether they found it
- **Price they pay or have paid for software** (not their capital)
- **Their words, verbatim** — the phrases that will become landing copy
- **What they'd be disappointed to lose** (the Sean Ellis question), if asked

**Never record against a name** (compliance rule 8): capital, account size, holdings, positions, experience level, risk tolerance, goals. If volunteered, hear it, don't write it. And never record the name either — see the public-repo warning at the top of this file.
